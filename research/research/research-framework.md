# Research Framework

## 1. Research Problem

Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) often operate with limited access to advanced financial forecasting and decision-support tools. Many rely on fragmented systems and reactive financial strategies, reducing operational efficiency and long-term resilience.

This research seeks to explore how AI-driven IT support systems can be designed to improve financial forecasting accuracy and decision-making processes for SMEs.

---

## 2. Research Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- To design a scalable AI-powered financial forecasting model tailored to SME constraints  
- To develop a structured system architecture integrating predictive analytics  
- To evaluate the feasibility of AI-enhanced financial decision-support tools  
- To assess performance using simulated financial datasets  

---

## 3. Methodological Approach

This project follows a structured design-oriented methodology:

1. Literature review on AI applications in financial management  
2. System architecture conceptualization  
3. Model selection and prototype development  
4. Simulation-based testing using synthetic datasets  
5. Performance evaluation and iterative refinement  

---

## 4. Expected Contribution

This research aims to contribute:

- A structured AI-integrated financial management framework  
- A conceptual architecture model for SME-focused implementation  
- Prototype-level validation of predictive financial analytics for SMEs
